Learning storyline

From physical assumptions to defensible evidence

Each stage produces something students can inspect, challenge, and explain.

01

Build

Define the mesh, p+ emitter, n-type base, contacts, doping, and optical source.

02

Solve

Use continuation to reach equilibrium, dark bias, illumination, and the operating J–V curve.

03

Measure

Convert instrument current to current density with traceable area, polarity, units, and metadata.

04

Calibrate

Fit a limited parameter set, inspect residual blocks, and decide whether the result is identifiable.

Model scope

Simple enough to learn; explicit enough to critique

This is a transparent teaching model for undergraduate students. It is not an industrial process deck or a claim of fabrication-grade parameter extraction.

  • Ordinary p+-on-n junction, not a p–i–n device.
  • One-dimensional transport with stated boundary conditions.
  • Single-pass optical absorption; no rear reflector or light trapping.
  • Only parameters supported by the available observations are fitted.
