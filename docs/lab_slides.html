Tool foundation

DEVSIM in the Semiconductor TCAD Workflow

Device TCAD discretizes semiconductor conservation laws on a mesh and predicts internal fields and terminal measurements.

Physical inputsgeometry, materials, doping, contacts, sources
Discrete problemmesh, model expressions, equations, boundary conditions
Predicted outputspotential, carriers, currents, J–V, capacitance

Published DEVSIM capabilities

  • finite-volume device simulation in 1D, 2D and 3D
  • Python scripting and user-specified PDEs
  • DC, small-signal AC and transient analyses
  • Apache 2.0 source code with text-based model files

Why it fits this course

  • equations, parameters and solver steps remain visible
  • scripts and results can be version controlled
  • students can inspect spatial fields before terminal curves
  • the 1D model runs on ordinary student computers
Model responsibility: DEVSIM supplies numerical infrastructure. The user must define suitable physics, parameters, contacts, mesh and validation checks.

Sources: DEVSIM project README and DEVSIM Manual v2.10.0.

Abridged for teaching: the project also defines doping, material parameters, contact equations, optical generation and solver tolerances.

  1. Mesh: positions are in centimetres; tags attach regions and contacts to physical locations.
  2. Unknowns: Potential, Electrons and Holes become degrees of freedom on mesh nodes.
  3. Equations: helper functions register Poisson, continuity, flux and contact residuals.
  4. Solve: DEVSIM assembles the discrete residual and Jacobian, then iterates to a converged state.
Read every model script in this order: geometry → parameters → unknowns → equations → boundary conditions → solve → extract outputs.

Device orientation

Read the p⁺-on-n Cell from Front to Rear

  • Light enters through the shallow p⁺ emitter at x = 0
  • The depletion region extends around the 0.5 µm junction, mostly into the lower-doped n base
  • Electrons are minority carriers in p⁺; holes are minority carriers in the n base
Direction check: in the depletion region, the built-in field points from n to p (−x): holes drift toward the front and electrons toward the rear. Diffusion also matters in the quasi-neutral regions.
Before solving: sketch the sign of net doping and electric field.

Physical model

Governing Equations Solved by DEVSIM

Electrostatics

∇·(ε∇ψ) = −q(p − n + ND − NA)

Charge sets potential, band bending and electric field.

Carrier conservation

∇·Jn = q(R − G),   ∇·Jp = −q(R − G)

G is the optical source; R includes SRH, Auger and effective near-contact loss regions.

Jn = qµn(nE + Vt∇n),    Jp = qµp(pE − Vt∇p)

Prescribed inputs

Geometry, doping, ε, µ, lifetime, T, optical G(x) and the applied contact voltage.

Solved unknowns

Potential ψ, electron density n and hole density p are coupled and solved self-consistently.

Calculated outputs

E, Jn, Jp, recombination, contact current, J–V and wavelength-by-wavelength EQE.

Why self-consistent? ψ changes carrier transport; n and p change charge; charge changes ψ. Solving these equations independently would break the physical feedback loop.

Complete mathematical problem

Boundary Conditions and Model Closure

Electrical contacts

Vback = 0,   V = Vfront − Vback n|c = neq,c,   p|c = peq,c
  • front and rear use ideal ohmic-contact equations
  • positive V forward biases the p⁺-on-n junction
  • equilibrium contact potentials follow local doping

Optical source and terminal map

Gλ(x) = Φλ(1 − Rf)αλe−αλx J = Jdevice(Vj) − Vj/Rsh Vterm = Vj − JRs,   I = AJ
  • light enters at x = 0 with no rear optical reflection
  • Rs, Rsh and area are applied outside the PDE solve
Boundary-condition count: Poisson and the two steady-state continuity equations need contact conditions at both ends. The ideal contact models close ψ, n and p at the front and rear.

From internal solution to measurement

Close the External Circuit: Define V, I and Power

What the simulator and experiment exchange

DEVSIM:   Vj → Jdevice(Vj) Terminal map:   J = Jdevice − Vj/Rsh Vterm = Vj − JRs,   I = AJ
  • Short circuit: Vterm = 0, current ≈ Isc
  • Open circuit: I = 0, voltage = Voc
  • Maximum power: choose the point maximizing VtermI
Do not mix levels: area, Rs and Rsh map internal current density to terminal measurements; they are not silicon PDE regions.

Numerical method

How the Continuous Equations Become a Computable Solve

1 · Finite volume

∫Ωᵢ∇·J dΩ = Ji+½ − Ji−½

Integrating each conservation law over a control volume makes current balance explicit at every node.

2 · Edge transport

Jn,i+½ = (qµnVT/Δx)[ni+1B(ξ) − niB(−ξ)],   ξ = Δψ/VT

The Scharfetter–Gummel Bernoulli form couples drift and diffusion stably across steep potential gradients.

3 · Newton update

JF(uk)Δu = −F(uk)

For u = [ψ, n, p], DEVSIM repeatedly assembles residuals and the Jacobian until the coupled equations converge.

EquilibriumPoisson only
Dark deviceadd n and p
Illuminateramp G gradually

A converged solution is necessary, not sufficient: also inspect residuals, repeat with a finer mesh and verify that extracted observables change negligibly.

Multi-observable calibration

Historical Cell #3: More Observables Expose Model Mismatch

python scripts/run_calibration.py --joint --sample 3
rb = √(wb/Nb) · [ysim,b − ymeas,b]/σb
  • Joint objective: illuminated J–V + dark J–V + independent Jsc and Voc
  • Block size Nb, weight wb and scale σb prevent a dense curve from dominating by point count alone

Fit diagnosis

The Quality Gate Fails Before Parameter Trust Begins

python scripts/run_calibration.py --joint --sample 3
Q = ΣbwbRMSb² / Σbwb = 15.1 > 4.0
  • largest mismatch: illuminated J–V block, normalized RMS ≈ 5.0
  • Voc check also disagrees by ≈ 4.5 declared scales
  • Q = 4 is a human-set teaching guard: weighted RMS mismatch ≈ 2 scales
Consequence: covariance is only local sensitivity information here—not evidence that the parameters are uniquely identified.
Next scientific action: inspect residual shape and measurement metadata; do not add many free parameters merely to force Q below the threshold.

Model limits and defensible claims

Separate Data, Model and Claim

Data

Preserve raw files; state units, illuminated area and sign convention. Experimental efficiency requires independently measured irradiance.

Model

1D abrupt junction, Boltzmann statistics, fixed mobility, single-pass Beer–Lambert optics and terminal Rs/Rsh; no band-gap narrowing, rear optical reflection, texture or lateral grid.

Claim

Fit only sensitive parameters. Show residuals, uncertainty and cross-observable checks; explain systematic mismatch.

Keep the inverse problem identifiable: a transparent two-parameter diagnosis is stronger than an elaborate unsupported fit.
